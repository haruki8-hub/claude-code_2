Our story

Bringing the workshop to you

Service Due is a local mobile vehicle servicing business based in Southern Tasmania. We started with a simple idea: getting your car serviced shouldn't cost you half a day.

Instead of dropping your car off, organising lifts and waiting around, we come to your home, workplace or site and do the job there. It's the same quality you'd expect from a good workshop, without the hassle.

Behind every job is more than 40 years of hands-on automotive experience, from everyday family cars and work utes to the odd vintage tractor.
