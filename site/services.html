Our services

Everything your car needs, on-site

From routine logbook services to brakes and batteries, we bring the workshop to your driveway, car park or work site.

Logbook servicing

Servicing to your manufacturer's schedule using the right oils, filters and parts, which keeps your new-car warranty valid. We stamp your logbook on the spot and let you know about anything that needs attention.

Brakes

Brake inspections, pads and rotors. We use quality Repco parts that exceed OEM standards. If you prefer another brand, just let us know when booking and we'll do our best to source it.

Batteries

Battery health checks and replacements at your home or work. Ideal if your car is slow to start or has been sitting for a while.

General maintenance

Oil and filter changes, fluid top-ups, belts and hoses, lights, wipers, tyres and general safety checks: the regular maintenance that keeps small problems from becoming big ones.

Air conditioning

Air-conditioning checks and servicing so your car stays cool in summer and demists properly in winter.

Fleet & commercial

Keep your work vehicles on the road. We service utes, vans and fleet vehicles at your depot or site, scheduled around your business to minimise downtime.

Quality parts as standard

We use Repco parts that exceed OEM standards. Prefer a particular brand? Tell us when you book and we'll endeavour to source it.
