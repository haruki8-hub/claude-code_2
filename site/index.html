FAQ

Common questions

Will a mobile service void my new-car warranty?

No. We service to your manufacturer's logbook schedule with quality parts and stamp your logbook, so your warranty stays intact.

What do you need from me?

Just a safe, flat spot to work, like a driveway, carport or car park, and access to the car's keys.

Do you service work vehicles and fleets?

Yes. Utes, vans and fleet vehicles can be serviced at your depot or work site to keep downtime low.

What parts do you use?

Repco parts that exceed OEM standards. Prefer another brand? Let us know when booking and we'll source it.
