What we do

Servicing & repairs, wherever you are

Everything your vehicle needs to stay safe and on the road, done properly in your driveway or car park.

Logbook servicing

Manufacturer-schedule servicing that keeps your new-car warranty intact, with your logbook stamped on the spot.

Brakes

Pads, rotors and brake inspections, fitted with quality Repco parts that exceed OEM standards.

Batteries

Battery testing and replacement wherever you are, so a flat battery doesn't leave you stranded.

General maintenance

Oil and filter changes, fluids, belts, hoses and safety checks to keep everything running right.

Air conditioning

Air-con checks and servicing so your car stays comfortable, whatever the Tassie weather.

Utes, vans and work vehicles serviced on-site with minimal downtime. Fleet enquiries welcome.
